Inverse kinematics — drag to reach

Click or drag inside the box. The real C++ damped-least-squares solver (compiled to WebAssembly) finds joint angles that put the arm's tip on your cursor, warm-started from the current pose — exactly the algorithm in ik/dls.hpp, position-only so a 2-link arm can track a 2-D target. The red ring is your target; the blue arm is the solution.
